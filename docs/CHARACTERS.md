# Player characters: concepts

Draft concepts for unique player characters, replacing the generic "demon hunter" so far.
v1 ships one character (see SPEC); the rest are candidates for later and for loadout-code
allies in night-night.

Every concept is built to read at 48×48 pixels: one big silhouette hook (a hat, cape or
prop shape you can recognise from its outline alone) and one accent colour. Each leans
toward a path without locking it, and has a capstone special for its upgrade tree.

## 1. Hotaru, the Lantern Warden

- **Who:** youngest warden of the clan that lights the paper lanterns marking held ground.
  Cheerful, stubborn, talks to her lantern.
- **Silhouette hook:** a wide, flat straw kasa hat, and a glowing paper lantern hanging from
  a pole over her shoulder.
- **Accent:** warm amber lantern glow against an indigo kimono and hakama.
- **Leans toward:** Fire and Spirit.
- **Capstone special:** *Lantern Vigil*: plants her lantern; inside its light, enemies slow
  and she heals a little. A small piece of held ground, matching the setting.

## 2. Kuro, the Crow Ronin

- **Who:** a masterless swordsman raised by crow tengu. Quiet, quick, a bit of a show-off.
- **Silhouette hook:** a ragged straw-and-feather mino cloak that flares like crow wings, and
  a half tengu mask with a short beak.
- **Accent:** black and deep blue, with a single red cord on the sword.
- **Leans toward:** Thunder and Beast.
- **Capstone special:** *Murder of Crows*: the dodge roll bursts into crows that peck
  everything they pass through.

## 3. Yuki, the Fox-Mask Shrine Maiden

- **Who:** a shrine maiden who folds her own paper familiars. Gentle with spirits, fierce
  with demons.
- **Silhouette hook:** a white fox mask pushed up on her head (its ears break the outline),
  very wide sleeves, and paper talismans circling her.
- **Accent:** red hakama and white, with blue frost glints.
- **Leans toward:** Frost and Spirit; strongest with shikigami.
- **Capstone special:** *Hundred Talismans*: every shikigami fires at once while a ring of
  talismans freezes enemies near her.

## 4. Gonta, the Oni-Blooded Brawler

- **Who:** a big-hearted kid born with one small oni horn, raised by the clan anyway. Loud,
  loyal, always hungry.
- **Silhouette hook:** oversized iron gauntlets that make his arms look huge, one stubby
  horn, and a rope belt with a gourd.
- **Accent:** orange-red skin tone on one arm (the oni side), green tunic.
- **Leans toward:** Beast and Fire; the tanky pick.
- **Capstone special:** *Oni Awakening*: for a few seconds the horn glows, hits can't be
  interrupted and every finisher becomes the big charge finisher.

## 5. Mei & Pom, the Paper Folder and her Tanuki

- **Who:** a cheerful 8-year-old origami master and Pom, the chubby tanuki who rides on her
  shoulders. Designed for an 8-year-old player: bright, friendly and impossible to lose on screen.
- **Silhouette hook:** two big round hair buns with yellow ribbons, and Pom's round body,
  ears and big striped tail on her shoulders. She holds a large paper crane.
- **Accent:** bright magenta kimono with white cherry blossoms and a sky-blue sash, the
  strongest colours of any character so she stands out against grass and at night.
- **Leans toward:** Beast, with shikigami support.
- **Capstone special:** *Shape-Shift*: Pom turns into a giant paper tanuki that charges
  through the parade.

## Generating them

Each concept becomes a 48×48 sprite request facing down-right, like the other templates.
Retro Diffusion's Pro model can take up to 9 reference images, which helps keep one
character consistent later across the animation frames in phase 2.
